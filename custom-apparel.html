Custom Apparel FAQ

What's the minimum for custom shirts?

Screen printing starts at 24 pieces per design. Embroidery and DTG runs start at 12, and we can quote single-piece samples.

Can I mix sizes in one order?

Yes. Size assortment within the same garment and color is free. Mixed colors are usually fine with the same imprint.

Which brands can you print on?

Bella+Canvas, Next Level, Gildan, Comfort Colors, Carhartt, Nike, Under Armour, Port Authority, Dickies, Champion and more.

How long does custom apparel take?

7–14 business days after proof approval for most orders. Rush and 3-day turnaround is available on stock blanks.

Is embroidery or printing better for uniforms?

Embroidery for polos, hats and workwear that gets washed constantly; screen printing for tees, hoodies and large chest or back graphics.
