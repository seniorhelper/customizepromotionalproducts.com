Custom Drinkware FAQ

Are your custom tumblers dishwasher safe?

Laser-engraved stainless tumblers are dishwasher safe. Printed and powder-coated styles are best hand-washed to protect the finish; we'll flag which is which on your proof.

What's the minimum order for custom drinkware?

Premium tumblers and mugs start at 24–36 pieces. Can coolers and plastic bottles typically start at 100.

Can you match my brand color on a tumbler?

Yes. Powder-coated tumblers come in dozens of stock colors and we can match Pantone on larger runs. Your logo can be engraved or printed on top.

How long does drinkware take?

7–14 business days after proof approval for most orders, plus transit. Rush options exist on stock colors.

Can I mix sizes or colors in one order?

Usually. Many suppliers allow color assortments within one imprint at the same total quantity — ask us on your quote.
