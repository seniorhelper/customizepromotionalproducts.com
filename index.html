Customize Promotional Products: FAQ

What does it cost to customize promotional products?

Costs vary by item, quantity and decoration method. Custom pens with a logo typically start under a dollar per unit at higher quantities, logo water bottles run a few dollars to the high teens, and branded apparel runs roughly eight to forty dollars. Larger orders unlock lower per-unit pricing, and every quote is free.

What is the minimum order for custom promotional items?

Most items have minimums between 12 and 250 units depending on the product. Pens often start around 250, drinkware around 24, apparel around 12 to 24, and tents and displays can be ordered as a single piece.

How long does it take to get promotional products with my logo?

Standard production runs about 7 to 14 business days after proof approval, plus shipping. Rush production is available on many items when you have a tight deadline.

Can I see a proof before I order?

Yes. Every order includes a free digital proof showing placement, size and color. Nothing goes into production until you approve it.

What logo files do you need?

Vector files (AI, EPS, SVG or PDF) are best. High-resolution PNG or JPG files work for most methods, and if your logo is low resolution our team can redraw it at no charge.

Which promotional products work best for small business?

Useful everyday items win: pens, drinkware, tote bags, hats and apparel. They cost little per impression because people keep and use them for months or years.

What decoration methods do you offer?

Screen printing, embroidery, pad printing, laser engraving, full-color digital and UV printing, sublimation and direct-to-garment. We recommend the right method for each product on your proof.

Do you offer eco-friendly promotional products?

Yes. Recycled, organic, biodegradable and sustainably sourced options are available across bags, drinkware, pens and apparel.

Can you handle ongoing programs, not just one-time orders?

Yes. Beyond event swag, we run onboarding kits, client gifting, uniforms and recognition programs, including kitting and drop-shipping to individual recipients.

Where do you ship?

Gnomad Promotions is based in the Dallas–Fort Worth area and ships custom promotional products to all 50 states.
