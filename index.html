Decoration Methods

How Your Logo Actually
Gets Onto the Product.

The decoration method decides how sharp your logo looks, how long it survives, and what the setup costs. Six methods cover almost every order we place. Knowing which one applies to your product is the difference between a quote you can compare and a quote you have to trust.

Screen Printing

Ink pushed through a mesh stencil, one screen per color. The workhorse for flat fabric at volume.

Best for
T-shirts, tote bags, drawstring bags
Colors
Spot colors — each color is a separate setup
Economics
Setup per color, then very low per-unit cost. Wins above ~72 pieces.

Embroidery

Thread stitched directly into the garment. Reads as the most premium option and outlasts the fabric.

Best for
Hats, polos, fleece, outerwear, bag panels
Colors
Thread-matched to Pantone; gradients do not translate
Economics
One-time digitising fee, then priced by stitch count

Pad Printing

A silicone pad lifts ink from an etched plate and lays it onto a curved or irregular surface.

Best for
Pens, keychains, bottle openers, small hard goods
Colors
1 – 4 spot colors, small imprint area
Economics
Cheapest method on small items; low setup

Laser Engraving

A laser removes the surface layer to expose the base material. No ink, so nothing to fade, chip, or wash off.

Best for
Stainless drinkware, metal pens, bamboo, leather
Colors
Single tone — the natural color of the substrate
Economics
No color setups; permanent mark; premium perception

Digital & UV Printing

Full-color inkjet cured under UV light, printed directly onto hard goods including wrap-around coverage.

Best for
Tumblers, tech accessories, notebooks, coasters
Colors
Unlimited — photographs and gradients reproduce cleanly
Economics
No per-color charge; strongest option for complex logos

Direct-to-Garment (DTG)

Water-based ink jetted straight into cotton fibres. Full color with no screens and no color-count penalty.

Best for
Cotton tees at low quantities, detailed or photographic artwork
Colors
Unlimited; softest hand-feel on light garments
Economics
No setup — wins below ~48 pieces, loses to screen print above it
