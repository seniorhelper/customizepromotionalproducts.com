How We Decorate Custom Dresses

We match the decoration method to the material and your budget, and explain why on your proof.

Screen Printing

Bold, durable ink at the best price once quantities climb past a couple dozen pieces.

Embroidery

Stitched logos that read premium on polos, jackets and workwear and survive years of washing.

DTF & Direct-to-Garment

Full-color and small-run printing with no screen setup, great for detailed artwork.

Custom Dresses FAQ

What is the minimum order for custom dresses?

Most decorated dresses start around 12 to 24 pieces per design, and some styles can be sampled as a single piece. Your quote confirms the exact minimum for the style you pick.

Can I mix sizes and colors on custom dresses?

Yes. Mixing sizes within one garment and color is standard, and many styles allow color assortments under the same imprint.

Will I see a proof of my dresses before production?

Always. Every order of dresses includes a free digital proof showing logo placement, size and color. Nothing prints until you approve it.
