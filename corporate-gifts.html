Corporate Gifts FAQ

Can you ship gifts directly to each recipient?

Yes. Send us a spreadsheet of names and addresses and we kit, box and drop-ship each gift with a personalized card.

What's a good budget for a client gift?

Most B2B client gifts land between $40 and $120 per recipient. Executive and top-account gifts often run $150–$300.

How far ahead should I order holiday gifts?

Lock in by mid-October for December delivery. Kitting and custom packaging add 1–2 weeks on top of product production.

Can you add personalized names to each gift?

Yes — engraved names, printed cards and variable-data labels are all available on kit orders.

Do you offer gifts under $20?

Absolutely. Branded notebooks, mugs, pens and snack boxes make great high-volume gifts for larger lists.
